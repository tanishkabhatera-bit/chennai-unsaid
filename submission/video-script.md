# Demo video script

The full script, with every line, clip and edit, is in [recording-plan.md](recording-plan.md).

Rules that shape the video:
- YouTube, 3 minutes or less, Public or Unlisted, plays in a signed-out browser.
- "Your project has to use AWS, and your demo video has to show it." AWS gets about 30 seconds in the AWS console.
- Judges only score what the video shows.
- Being on camera isn't required. The face clips are there because they make the opening land.
